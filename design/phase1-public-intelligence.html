CPSC expands third-party testing to imported children's sleepwear

EFFECTIVE 2026-09-15 PUBLISHED 2026-07-22 LAST REVIEWED 2026-07-30 AUTHORITY CPSC — federal

What changed

The Commission extended the third-party testing requirement under 16 CFR Part 1615 to cover imported finished sleepwear garments in children's sizes 0–14, closing the prior exemption for garments tested at the fabric stage only.

Children's Product Certificates issued against fabric-stage testing before 2026-09-15 do not satisfy the new requirement and are not carried over.

Who it hits

  • Toys & Children's Products — importers of finished sleepwear.
  • Apparel & Accessories — sellers listing children's sleepwear in sizes 0–14.
  • Not affected — adult sizes, non-sleepwear children's apparel, and domestic manufacturers already testing finished garments.

Reviewed action template

Available because this record has reviewed primary-official evidence and a reviewed action template. Templates are general guidance, not legal advice.

  • Identify listings in children's sizes 0–14 classified as sleepwear.
  • Ask your testing lab whether existing certificates were issued at fabric or finished-garment stage.
  • Re-certify finished garments before 2026-09-15 where only fabric-stage testing exists.

Evidence

Every conclusion above rests on these records.
