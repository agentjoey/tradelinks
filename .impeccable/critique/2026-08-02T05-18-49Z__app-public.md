## What's Working

1. The Monitored card is the contract's hardest requirement done right — the limit is a full sentence beginning with what cannot be done, bordered urgent, naming mechanism and consequence; Verified evidence opens inline.
2. The accessibility floor is real: skip link is the first Tab stop and visibly focuses (verified live), clean h1→h2→h3, readiness never colour-alone, light default SSR'd as `data-theme="light"`.
3. The honesty system is systemic — three different components, one voice.
